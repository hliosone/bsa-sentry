IF SOMETHING GOES WRONG
======================================================================
- Claude's request fails, "Flex locked": unlock the Flex, open BSA Sentry, ask again.
- Status bar says another tab took over: close the other BSA Sentry tabs, reload.
- The Flex asks "Budget exceeded" at the end: the budget was left at 20. Tap "Pay once"
  and keep going, or redo from scene 3 with 30.
- The limit runs low: Flex menu, "+20 USDC (reset budget)", then retake.
- Backup fails with "unexpected status word (0x6f00)": the Flex holds 6 trusted merchants.
  Flex: gear, Trusted merchants, remove Restaurant (confirm), then Back up now again.
- Any scene fails: just redo that scene. Payments already made stay on chain.
